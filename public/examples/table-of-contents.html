    Introduction

    The library started as three components and a colour ramp. Everything here follows from that constraint: one accent, two radii, and motion only where it explains a change of state.

    How it works

    Each component owns its own stylesheet and ships with no dependencies. The shared layer holds tokens, reset and the motion primitives — nothing that a component can opt out of.

    Spacing is a 4px scale, radii are capped at 22px, and every interactive surface has a visible focus ring.

    Summary

    Twenty-eight components, one design language, zero runtime dependencies. Copy the files, or take the zip.
